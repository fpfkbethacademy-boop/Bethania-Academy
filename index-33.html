Why Families Choose Us

A legacy of excellence, since 1992

Founded by the FPFK Likoni Church Women's Fellowship, Bethania Academy has grown from nine learners into a leading educational hub in Mombasa County — and parents across the area consistently tell us why they consider us the best primary and junior school in Likoni.

Academic giants

75% of our 2025 Grade 9 learners joined Cluster 1 schools, with our top student scoring 70/72 points — the best performing school in Likoni Sub-County.

Co-curricular kings

Renowned for our award-winning traditional and modern dance troupes, our Sea Scouts and our football teams, consistently taking top honours in regional competitions.

A caring community

Through our partnership with the Aga Khan Foundation and the local FPFK Church, we support needy students with fees and nutrition every year.

Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

Everything families usually ask before joining Bethania — if you don't see your question here, reach us on WhatsApp any time.

How do I apply for admission?
Start your application on our Admissions page or book a campus visit via WhatsApp. Our team will guide you through the requirements for Pre-Primary, Primary or Junior Secondary placement.
What curriculum does Bethania Academy follow?
We follow Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) from Pre-Primary through Junior Secondary (Grade 9), with a strong record of learners progressing into Cluster 1 secondary schools.
How can I access my child's report card and fee statement?
Parents and guardians can log in to the Secure Academic Portal at any time to view report cards, fee statements, attendance and assignments. If you don't yet have login details, message us on WhatsApp and we'll set up your account.
Do you support needy or bursary students?
Yes. Through our partnership with the Aga Khan Foundation and the local FPFK Church, we've supported over 400 needy students with fees and nutrition since 1992.
Where is the school located?
We're along Likoni–Ukunda Road, Mombasa, Kenya (P.O. BOX 96066 - 80100). Call us on +254 711 638 425 or chat on WhatsApp to arrange a visit.
