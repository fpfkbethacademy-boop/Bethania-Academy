Why Families Choose Us

A legacy of excellence, since 1992

Founded by the FPFK Likoni Church Women's Fellowship, Bethania Academy has grown from nine learners into a leading educational hub in Mombasa County.

Academic giants

75% of our 2025 Grade 9 learners joined Cluster 1 schools, with our top student scoring 70/72 points — the best performing school in Likoni Sub-County.

Co-curricular kings

Renowned for our award-winning traditional and modern dance troupes, consistently taking top honours in regional competitions.

A caring community

Through our partnership with the Aga Khan Foundation and the Local FPFK Church, we support needy students with fees and nutrition every year.
